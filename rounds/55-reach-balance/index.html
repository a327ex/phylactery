The snake that reached the egg

Your run, replayed headless. Since the replay only holds what was drawn, I reproduced it in the live game: the lich at eight spots one to one and a half metres from the egg, packs and spreads coming every 5 seconds. On the code you played, that gave 2 snakes at the egg in 16 minutes, and the record of each snake’s last 3 seconds showed both happening the same way, most likely how yours got through too.

A thrown rock or the lens had picked the snake out early. While a rock or lens is on its way to a snake, the other attacks leave that snake to it until it gets close (1.5 m from the egg for a rock, 2 m for the lens), so they don’t pile onto the same snake. That time, the rock or lens didn’t get there in time. When the snake came that close, an urgent gesture started (a beam once, a lift once), but an urgent gesture still raised the arm and waited for a short beat, up to about half a second. A snake that close needs only about a third of a second to reach the egg.

The fixes: a gesture with a snake within 1.6 m of the egg goes off at once, the arm wherever it is (the beam and the lift take their snake the moment they go off). And behind everything, a last resort: any snake still loose within 1.35 m is taken there and then by whatever takes at once, whatever else is going on: the fire leaps to it, or a lift, or a beam shot. The same stress, 48 minutes of it, 3 times as long: no snake at the egg, and the last resort never had to act.

The orbs: much fainter marks

The lines are less than half as wide and about a third as strong: faint frosted lines in the glass rather than bands.

The name holds still

The name used to sit under the lowest point of the object as it turned, so it rose and fell 8 to 30 pixels with every turn. Now it sits under the space the object turns within, which is the same whichever way it faces: measured in every shot of all six objects at three angles, the name is on exactly the same row. Only zooming moves it now. Because each object is framed to fill the same space, the name sits at the same height for all six.
